Signature Dishes & Specialties

Our most popular Maharashtrian preparations made fresh daily with slow-roasted spices, stone-ground masalas, and authentic Kolhapuri recipes.

Signature Food • खास पदार्थ

Our Most Popular Maharashtrian Dishes

Prepared fresh daily with roasted spices, stone-ground aromatics, and authentic Kolhapuri & Desi culinary methods.

Bestseller • प्रसिद्ध थाळी

Chicken Masala Thali

Our signature thali featuring succulent chicken in roasted masala, a signature whole chicken leg piece, unlimited hot Kala Rassa, warm Jowar/Bajra Bhakri or Chapati, and Indrayani rice topped with pure ghee.

Complete Meal ₹380 – ₹400
House Special • मटन थाळी

Mutton Masala Thali

Tender, slow-cooked goat meat simmered in authentic dark roasted Kala Masala gravy. Served with fiery hot rassa, hand-made bhakris, fresh onions, lemon, and fragrant Indrayani rice.

Slow-Cooked Mutton ₹580 – ₹600
Spicy Roast • चिकन सुक्का

Chicken Fry (Sukka)

Pan-roasted chicken pieces tossed vigorously with charred onion-coconut paste, garlic, ginger, and coarse Maharashtrian spices. Crisp on edges and intensely flavorful.

Pan-Fried Sukka ₹260 – ₹280
Desi Favourite • मटन फ्राय

Mutton Sukka / Fry

Tender chunks of mutton dry-roasted with black spices, fresh coriander, and curry leaves. Best enjoyed paired with hot Jowar Bhakri and a side of piping hot rassa.

Dry Roasted ₹360 – ₹380
Signature Broth • काळा रस्सा

Traditional Kala Rassa

The soul of Sainath Khanawal — a dark, aromatic broth prepared by slow-roasting dry copra (coconut) and onions until blackened, ground with whole garam masalas and simmered with meat stock.

Unlimited with Thali ₹80 (Bowl)
Made on Order • ज्वारी/बाजरी भाकरी

Fresh Jowar & Bajra Bhakri

Hand-patted round breads made from nutritious sorghum (jowar) or pearl millet (bajra) flour, puffed over direct flames. Soft inside with a lightly crisp crust.

Flame-Roasted Bread ₹30 – ₹40
