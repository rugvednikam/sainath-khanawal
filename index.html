About the Khanawal • आमची ओळख

A Hearty Pune Khanawal Tradition in Sadashiv Peth

Located right near Alka Chowk and Pantancha Gate at the start of Kumthekar Road, Sainath Khanawal (साईनाथ खानावळ — नादखुळा मराठमोळं) has long been a beloved dining landmark for Pune locals seeking authentic, spicy Maharashtrian non-vegetarian meals.

We prepare our food the authentic local way — with slow-roasted dry copra (coconut), caramelized onions, whole spices, and freshly ground Kala Masala. Every thali is served piping hot, generous in portion, and packed with true regional flavours.

Whole Leg Piece in Every Chicken Thali

Every chicken thali includes a hearty, juicy chicken leg piece slow-simmered in rich masala gravy.

Unlimited Spicy Kala & Tambda Rassa

Hot, aromatic, slow-simmered broth refilled freely at your table to enjoy with every bite.

Hand-Patted Bhakris & Indrayani Ghee Rice

Hot jowar or bajra bhakris straight off the flame, paired with indigenous Indrayani rice topped with pure sajuk tup (ghee).

Khanawal Dining Guide & House Customs

A few helpful pointers for first-time and visiting diners to enjoy the best experience.

०१

Single Diner Per Thali

To ensure unlimited rassa refills and fresh piping-hot servings, thalis are served for individual diners in traditional khanawal style.

०२

Made-to-Order Bhakris

Every Bhakri is hand-patted and baked fresh on the flame. During peak rush, please allow 5–10 minutes for your hot bread to arrive.

०३

Peak Timing & Seating

Weekend lunch (1:00 PM – 2:30 PM) sees heavy rush. Arriving early around 12:15 PM or 7:30 PM ensures quicker seating.

०४

Parking & Payment

Two-wheeler parking is available along Kumthekar Road. We accept Cash and all UPI payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm QR) at the counter.
