About Sainath Khanawal

Two decades of serving pure, unadulterated Maharashtrian flavours in Sadashiv Peth, Pune. Honest food, hearty portions, and timeless rustic traditions.

OUR CULINARY ORIGINS

What is a Khanawal? (खानावळ संस्कृती)

In Maharashtra, a Khanawal is not an ordinary commercial restaurant or diner. It is a traditional home-style eating house rooted in the warmth of communal kitchens.

Originally established in the late 19th and early 20th centuries to feed college students, workers, and traders living away from home, khanawals earned a sacred reputation: hot, wholesome, freshly cooked food served generously with unlimited gravy and warm bhakris.

Located right at the threshold of Sadashiv Peth near Alka Talkies junction, Sainath Khanawal has stood as a guardian of this authentic Puneri tradition for over twenty years.

OUR CULINARY CODE

The Four Kitchen Pillars

How we maintain authentic home-style flavors day in and day out.

Fresh Slow-Roasted Masalas

We never use packaged premixes. Our famous Kala Masala is created by dark roasting whole spices, dry coconut flakes, and dagad phool over low flame daily.

Piped Fresh Hand-Patted Bhakris

Every Jowar and Bajra Bhakri is kneaded with warm water upon order, patted entirely by hand, and roasted on heavy iron tawas until blistered and puffed.

Select Quality Country Cuts

We carefully inspect and select fresh daily meat cuts and authentic Gavran (free-range country chicken) for unmatched texture and robust broth depth.

Generous Puneri Hospitality

Unlimited Kala Rassa refills are an inviolable house custom. When you sit in our khanawal, you are fed like family until your soul is content.
