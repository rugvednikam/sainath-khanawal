Why Food Lovers Visit Sainath Khanawal

A no-frills, honest Maharashtrian dining experience built around pure taste.

Authentic Kala Masala Taste

We follow traditional slow-roasting techniques with stone-ground dry coconut and whole spices for a rich, unmistakable regional flavor profile.

Generous Portions & Whole Pieces

Known for hearty servings with tender meat pieces and signature chicken leg pieces in thalis that leave you fully satisfied.

Unlimited Piping Hot Rassa

Enjoy free refills of our flavorful spicy Kala and Tambda Rassa right at your table with your choice of Bhakri and Indrayani rice.

Honest, Pocket-Friendly Dining

No corporate fluff or high markup — just hearty, delicious non-vegetarian meals accessible for students, families, and foodies alike.
